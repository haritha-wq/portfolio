HR Analytics · Python · SciPy
05
HR Attrition Analysis — IBM HR Analytics

Rigorous statistical analysis of 1,470 employees (IBM HR Analytics) revealing a 24.0% attrition rate. Chi-square testing identified overtime as the dominant predictor (χ²=94.5, Cramer's V=0.25, p<0.0001) — overtime workers depart at 41.8% versus 17.3%, a 2.4× multiplier. Three evidence-based HR policy interventions proposed: overtime capping with compensatory leave, early-tenure retention milestones, and satisfaction-score auto-escalation.
